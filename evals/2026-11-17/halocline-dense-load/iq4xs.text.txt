B-trees are balanced tree structures that maintain data in a sorted order on disk, allowing for efficient random access and updates by modifying nodes in place. In contrast, Log-Structured Merge (LSM) trees optimize write performance by appending new data to an in-memory buffer and periodically flushing it to disk as sorted files, which are later merged to maintain consistency.
